Operation: Black Signal

Operation: Black Signal

In progress

Roblox Studio · Team project · 5 members

A team-built tactical shooter in Roblox Studio, where Task Force goes up against the Syndicate. We shared our progress in a video presentation.

My contribution: I was the programmer and map designer for our five-person team. I wrote the scripts that make the gameplay work and designed the map where the matches take place, making sure my code and level layout fit together with the UI, weapons, and the other parts built by my teammates.

Challenges and what I learned

  • With five people on one game, my scripts and map had to stay compatible with everyone else’s work, so we agreed early on how each part connects.
  • Designing the map taught me that layout shapes gameplay: routes, cover, and sightlines decide whether a fight feels fair for both Task Force and the Syndicate.
  • Building features one small step at a time and playtesting often helped me catch bugs early, before they affected the rest of the team.
